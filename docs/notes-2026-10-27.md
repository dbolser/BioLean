# Notes, 2026-10-27: belief framework, canon, ILP, and a textbook milestone

Discussion notes. Companion to [case-studies.md](case-studies.md) and
[formalisms/README.md](formalisms/README.md).

## 1. The two halves

Transcribing a paper into BioLean has two parts, and they fail differently.

1. **Labelling**: turning sentences into typed, grounded Statements. This is the analogue of
   transcribing a maths paper into Lean. The formalism survey covers it.
2. **Weighing**: the opinion attached to each Statement and how opinions combine. This is where
   BioLean has no Mathlib analogue, because a Lean theorem is simply true once checked.

## 2. What the belief framework commits us to

The weighing half is Jøsang's **subjective logic**, a specific small theory.

- A binomial opinion is (b, d, u): belief, disbelief, uncertainty, summing to 1. Contested is
  high b and high d; unknown is high u. These are different states, which is the reason for three
  numbers rather than one probability.
- A fourth number, the base rate a, turns an opinion into a probability: P = b + a·u. `Opinion.prob`
  defaults a = 1/2 and nothing sets it per claim. That is a gap: the prior for "drug X affects
  process Y" is nowhere near 1/2.
- An opinion is a Beta distribution in disguise. With r supporting and s refuting observations and
  prior weight W = 2: b = r/(r+s+W), d = s/(r+s+W), u = W/(r+s+W). Cumulative fusion is adding r
  and s. So the reliability table is a table of **pseudo-counts**: an RCT at 0.85 is 11 independent
  observations, a meta-analysis at 0.9 is 18. That is the honest way to argue about the weights.
- Our `chain` is not Jøsang's deduction operator. Deduction needs conditional opinions both ways
  and a base rate. Ours multiplies belief, sets disbelief to zero and puts the rest in uncertainty.
  It is a sound lower bound (proved never to exceed the weaker link) and it discards disbelief in
  the links. That is the "chains hide conflict" gap.
- Not yet used, but in the theory and matching named gaps: **averaging fusion** for non-independent
  sources (citation chains, reviews restating trials); **discounting** for trust in a source
  (mouse-to-human transfer, extraction reliability separate from study strength); **multinomial
  opinions** for "which of three rival mechanisms" rather than yes/no per claim.
- Rejected alternatives: Dempster-Shafer (same masses, worse combination under conflict), plain
  Beta-Bayesian updating (same maths, no explicit uncertainty), Pollock-style degrees of
  justification.

The kernel certifies the arithmetic, never the 0.85.

## 3. How canon bubbles up

A textbook sentence like "DNA is transcribed to RNA" is a **universal** claim: the same claim at
the widest context (NCBITaxon:131567, cellular organisms). Instances are specific: this gene, this
organism. Specialising downward is free. Generalising upward is a derivation rule, `generalize`,
whose opinion is the fused instance opinions **discounted by coverage** of the target context, not
by count. Details and numbers in case-studies.md, case 4. Three points from the discussion:

- Canon has two sources that should agree: bottom-up generalisation, and top-down assertion by a
  textbook or a GO definition (a `canonical` evidence kind). GO:0006351 "DNA-templated
  transcription" has the fact in its definition, so part of canon lives in the ontology as axioms,
  as Mathlib definitions do. Where the textbook asserts what generalisation cannot reach, that
  mismatch is a finding.
- Promotion to biolib is a threshold on belief and coverage, after which `axioms` stops expanding,
  like using a Mathlib lemma without re-checking Mathlib. It stays expandable.
- Exceptions are handled by context, not by weight. Retroviral reverse transcription refutes
  "information flows only DNA → RNA → protein" and leaves "DNA is transcribed to RNA in cellular
  organisms" untouched, because viruses lie outside the context.

Induction never closes: `uncertainty_pos` is a type invariant, so a universal is never proved, only
established. The coverage measure is a stated judgment. Clade fraction is too harsh (five model
organisms leave transcription "unsupported"); phylogenetic bracketing is what biologists do and the
taxonomy tree makes it computable.

## 3a. Formal systems for conditioning ("in mice", "in human prostate cancer")

Discussed 2026-10-26. Two layers of prior work: what the context *slots* are, and what
conditioning *means* logically.

**Slots.** Four systems converged on nearly the same list.

| system | slots | where they live |
|---|---|---|
| GO annotation extensions (Huntley et al. 2014, GAF column 16) | `occurs_in` (CL/UBERON/GO CC), `part_of`, `happens_during`, `has_input` | per annotation, RO relations |
| GO-CAM | the same as OWL individuals, plus `in_taxon` | per model |
| INDRA BioContext | species, cell line, cell type, organ, disease, location | per **evidence item**, not per statement |
| BEL annotations; SIGNOR | Species, Tissue, Disease, CellLine; tax_id, cell, tissue | per statement block |
| Biolink qualifiers (Translator) | species_context, anatomical_context, disease_context, stage, onset, sex, population_context, frequency | per association, but class-bound (not every edge type allows every qualifier) |

Biolink's is the best-maintained list because Translator forced a dozen groups to agree; start
from it. INDRA's per-evidence placement is right: the same claim has a different context in each
paper. The formalism survey confirmed the shared gap: none has dose, duration, age or strain.

**Semantics.**

- *Description logic / OWL.* A claim is a class expression: "endurance increase occurs_in some
  (organism and in_taxon Mus musculus)". Context behaves as an **existential**, never a universal:
  true in C57BL/6 implies true in some mice; true in mice does not imply true in mammals. So
  specialisation is free and generalisation is not. GO-CAM lives here; OWL reasoners do subsumption.
- *McCarthy's context logic, Cyc microtheories.* `ist(c, p)`: p holds in context c, with explicit
  **lifting rules** between contexts. "In mice" to "in humans" is a lifting rule and should carry its
  own reliability.
- *Transportability (Bareinboim & Pearl 2011, 2014).* The formal theory of when a causal effect
  measured in population A transfers to B, with **selection diagrams** encoding what differs and a
  do-calculus decision procedure. Mouse-to-human formalised; ends in yes/no plus a formula, i.e. a
  weighted edge in BioLean terms.
- *uPheno (Monarch).* Cross-species phenotype ontology mapping mouse MP to human HP via shared
  patterns, so "same claim, different species" is representable.
- *ECO.* Already names lifting evidence: "inferred from sequence orthology" (ISO) is a transport
  rule with an evidence code.

**Consequence for BioLean.** Claim becomes claim plus context, with context inside the claim.
Statements stay exactly as specific as the paper. Two new derivation constructors: `specialise`
(free) and `transport` (requires an evidence item of a new kind, low reliability, calibrated from
the animal-to-human translation literature). The NMN example then comes out right by construction:
established in mice, low belief in humans with the mouse papers listed as the axioms.

References: Huntley et al. 2014, BMC Bioinformatics 15:155; GO wiki "Occurs in";
Biolink 3.0 Migration Guide (github.com/biolink/biolink-model); Bareinboim & Pearl, "External
Validity: From Do-Calculus to Transportability Across Populations", Statistical Science 2014;
Bareinboim & Pearl, "Transportability of Causal Effects: Completeness Results", AAAI 2012;
McCarthy, "Notes on Formalizing Context", IJCAI 1993; Lenat, Cyc microtheories.

## 4. ILP is the inverse of a Derivation

Inductive logic programming (Muggleton; Progol, later Metagol, Popper, ILASP): given background B
and examples E, find H with B ∧ H ⊨ E. In BioLean, B is biolib, E is a set of established claims,
and H is the missing Statement that completes a Derivation. **A derivation with a hole is a
hypothesis.** Hypothesis generation becomes "which Statement, if it existed, would let this chain
type-check". Lean checks that; a search procedure performs it.

ILP's hypothesis space is the subsumption lattice, specific to general, walked by a refinement
operator; Plotkin's least general generalisation is the join. Progol constrained the space with
hand-written mode declarations. **The ontology is that lattice already**: climbing `is_a` in
NCBITaxon or UBERON is the generalisation step, `generalize` is lgg over contexts, and the typed
Derivation constructors play the role of mode declarations. So using the ontology as the hypothesis
space is a renaming, not a stretch goal.

Prior art to read: the Robot Scientist (King, Whelan, Muggleton et al., Nature 2004; "Adam",
Science 2009) used ILP over a yeast metabolic model with GO-style annotations to propose gene
function hypotheses and choose experiments, and had to score hypotheses against noisy evidence.
Tamaddoni-Nezhad used abductive ILP to fill gaps in metabolic networks. Ross King's group page:
https://www.ceb.cam.ac.uk/about/meet-our-academics/professor-ross-king/

## 5. Milestone: how much of a textbook can BioLean hold?

A bounded, canonical, public corpus is a better first target than any paper set. Candidates: the
AQA GCSE Biology specification (public, but AQA copyright) and OpenStax *Concepts of Biology*
(CC BY 4.0, introductory college level, close to GCSE/A-level content). Using OpenStax.

Measurement:

1. Split the text into atomic claims.
2. Ground every noun to CHEBI, GO, CL, UBERON, PATO, HP, NCBITaxon; type the relation as a Claim.
3. Bin each claim: expressible now; expressible with the Context extension; needs a construct we
   lack (name it); not a claim (procedure, "explain why", quantitative description, definition that
   belongs in the ontology).
4. Then try to **derive**: which textbook claims follow from others plus biolib, and what claim
   constructors the derivations need. This is the question that decides how expressive the core
   must be.

Prediction before running: entities ground above 80 percent; claims split roughly evenly between
effect-style statements we have, structural statements (part_of, made_of) we lack a constructor
for, and process descriptions that are really definitions and belong in the ontology layer. The
last bin says how much of canon is axioms versus theorems.
